# Privacy policy

Dojazd has no account, no server of its own, no analytics and no crash reporting. Its author receives nothing from the app: not who uses it, not where, not how.

## What leaves the phone

The app downloads public files straight from the transit operator of the chosen city:

- the timetable, when it is first set up and when it is checked for a newer one,
- delays, vehicle positions and service alerts, only while a screen that shows them is open.

These are the same whole-city files for everyone, so a request does not say which stop, line or journey you looked at. As with any connection, the operator's server sees your IP address, the time of the request and the name and version of the app. The operator's own privacy policy applies to that.

Every request the app makes is listed under Settings › Network log. In offline mode the app makes none.

Nothing else is sent anywhere. The map, the address search and the journey planner work on the phone, from files that come with the app.

## What stays on the phone

- **Favourite places** (such as home and work), until you delete them. They are written to a file outside the app only if you export them or choose a backup file yourself.
- **Recent journeys and recent searches**, a few of each. You can clear them, or switch history off, under Settings › History. The position of "My location" is not kept in them.
- **The network log**, up to 200 requests with their times. You can clear it.
- **Settings and the timetable.**

None of this is included in cloud backup. When you move to a new phone with Android's device-to-device transfer, it is copied along with the app's other data. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, removes all of it.

## Location

Your position is read from the phone's own location service only when you choose "My location". It is used on the phone to plan the journey and is not sent anywhere or written to disk.

## Keyboard

Unless you allow it under Settings › History, the app asks the keyboard not to learn from what you type into its search fields. Whether a keyboard honours that is up to the keyboard.

## Links

Links in service alerts and in the settings open in your browser. What happens there is outside the app.
